Put your own Pokemon Red ROM in this folder, named exactly:

    pokemon_red.gb

It must be the English (USA/Europe) release, 1 MB (1,048,576 bytes), SHA-256:

    5ca7ba01642a3b27b0cc0b5349b52792795b62d3ed977e98a09390659af96b7b

The build checks this hash: the compiled blocks (pre-translated game code) and the save logic
are made for that exact ROM. Then run ./build.sh from the repository root.

No ROM is included in this repository, and none will be provided. Use a dump of a cartridge
you own.
